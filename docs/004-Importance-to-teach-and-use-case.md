
# Importance to teach and use cases

## Project Context Analysis

### Target Audience Profile
- **Demographics**: 2000+ employees, many long-tenured and unfamiliar with AI
- **Current AI Exposure**: 82 Athena platform users (growing to hundreds)
- **Technical Background**: Non-technical business users
- **Learning Needs**: Foundational AI concepts, practical application skills
- **Attitude**: May be hesitant or skeptical about AI adoption

### Business Environment
- **Industry**: Investment management and financial services
- **Existing AI Platform**: Athena (in-house solution)
- **Current Use Cases**: Email generation, document analysis
- **Technology Stack**: Azure products (Power BI, Data Factory, Cloud Storage), Azure Databricks, internal Copilot tool

## Core AI Concepts to Teach

### 1. Foundational AI Understanding
- **What is AI vs. Machine Learning vs. Deep Learning**
  - Simple definitions with financial industry analogies
  - How AI differs from traditional software
  - Why AI is relevant to investment management

- **Types of AI Systems**
  - Generative AI (focus area for your project)

### 2. Large Language Models (LLMs) Can help...
- **How LLMs Work** (simplified explanation)
  - Training on vast text data
  - Pattern recognition and prediction
  - Limitations and "hallucinations"

- **Key Capabilities for non tech users**
  - Text generation and completion
  - Summarization and analysis of different files
  - Translation and formatting
  - Question answering

### 3. Prompt Engineering Essentials
- **Basic Prompt Structure**
  - Clear instructions
  - Context setting
  - Output format specification
  - Examples and templates

- **Advanced Techniques**
  - Chain-of-thought reasoning
  - Role-based prompting
  - Few-shot learning examples
  - Iterative refinement

### 4. AI Safety and Ethics
- **Data Privacy and Security**
  - What data AI systems can access
  - Confidentiality considerations
  - Regulatory compliance (financial industry specific)

- **Bias and Fairness**
  - How AI can perpetuate biases
  - Importance of diverse training data
  - Validation and testing approaches

## Use Cases for Federated Hermes Employees

### 1. Investment Research and Analysis
**Use Case**: Portfolio Manager Research Assistant
- **Application**: Summarize market research reports, extract key investment themes
- **AI Concepts**: Text summarization, information extraction, pattern recognition
- **Prompt Example**: "Analyze this earnings report and identify the top 3 risks and opportunities for long-term investors"

**Use Case**: ESG Analysis Automation
- **Application**: Extract ESG factors from company reports and news
- **AI Concepts**: Named entity recognition, classification, sentiment analysis
- **Business Value**: Faster ESG scoring, consistent analysis across companies

### 2. Client Communication and Reporting
**Use Case**: Personalized Client Update Generation
- **Application**: Create tailored portfolio updates based on client preferences
- **AI Concepts**: Template generation, personalization, data integration
- **Prompt Framework**: Context (client profile) + Data (portfolio performance) + Format (update style)

**Use Case**: Regulatory Compliance Documentation
- **Application**: Generate compliance reports and audit trails
- **AI Concepts**: Document generation, data validation, regulatory mapping
- **Business Value**: Reduced manual work, consistent formatting, audit readiness

### 3. Internal Operations and Productivity
**Use Case**: Meeting Summarization and Action Items
- **Application**: Convert meeting transcripts into structured summaries
- **AI Concepts**: Speech-to-text, summarization, task extraction
- **Implementation**: Integration with Teams/Zoom recordings

**Use Case**: Email Optimization and Response Drafting
- **Application**: Improve email clarity, tone, and effectiveness
- **AI Concepts**: Natural language processing, style transfer, sentiment analysis
- **Current State**: Already using email generation (expand and enhance)
